Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

After you place an order you’ll get a reference code and our bank transfer details. Once we confirm your payment, we email you a private link to build your invitation.

Can I edit after publishing?

Yes. Your private link keeps working, so you can edit anytime and re-publish. Your public link and slug stay the same.

What happens to my invitation after the wedding?

It stays live for 90 days after your wedding date, then automatically archives. You (or we) can adjust that date anytime.

Can guests RSVP and leave wishes?

Absolutely. Every invitation has a live RSVP form where guests enter their name and whether they can come, plus a guestbook for wishes. Every reply appears in your dashboard, and you can export all RSVPs to Excel.

Do you support Sinhala and Tamil?

Yes, guests can switch the interface between Sinhala, Tamil and English. Your own text stays exactly as you wrote it.
