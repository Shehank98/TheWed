    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I see a design before I pay?

    Yes — tap “Live preview” on any template to open the full, animated invitation. You only pay once you’ve chosen a design and are ready to build your own.

    How do I pay?

    After you place an order you’ll get a reference code and our bank transfer details. Once we confirm your payment, we send you a private link to build your invitation.

    Can I edit after publishing?

    Yes. Your private link keeps working, so you can edit anytime and re-publish. Your public link stays the same.

    Can guests add their own photos?

    Yes — every invitation has a live guest photo wall. Guests upload snapshots straight from their phones and they appear in your gallery. You can remove any you like.

    What happens after the wedding?

    Your invitation stays live for 90 days after your wedding date, then automatically archives. You (or we) can adjust that date anytime.
